AI in the business

An AI layer connected to the data of the whole business

This is how it works today at a client of mine, a company of 30 employees.

  • Ask in plain Hebrew An employee asks in plain Hebrew, for example "Which orders are delayed at a supplier?", and gets an answer based on the company's data.
  • A report in one click A useful report is saved, and can be run again with one click.
  • A dashboard from words A manager asks for a dashboard in words, and the system builds it.
  • One definition for every number Behind every number stands one agreed business definition, so everyone is talking about the same "sold" and the same "late".

Case study · Ida's Home

The whole business in one process, from sale to payment collection. And above it, an AI layer connected to every department.

Ida's Home designs and sells home furniture: made-to-order sofas and armchairs, lighting, rugs and textiles. The company has 30 employees, and works with several warehouses, a carpentry workshop and many suppliers.

  • One continuous process From sale to payment collection, instead of information scattered across people and files.
  • Updates from the field The warehouse, the couriers and sales update the system as they work, from their phones.
  • One picture for the whole company Every department sees the same order, in the same status, at the same time.
  • A reliable picture for management Of orders, inventory and debts. The system exposed gaps that had not been visible before, and they were dealt with.
