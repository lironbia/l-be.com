About the system

What is Kala CRM?

An Israeli system for managing, marketing and automating the business. Inquiries, customers, sales, billing, calendars and communication with customers, in one system and fully in Hebrew. In my view, one of the most advanced and comfortable systems in Israel.

  • Gathering and managing inquiries and leads from all sources
  • Customer database and customer files
  • Orders and sales processes
  • Quotes with automatic follow-up
  • Accounting documents
  • Credit card processing, digital wallets and direct debits (Masav, Israel's bank clearing system)
  • Subscriptions and recurring billing
  • Phone system (PBX), WhatsApp, SMS and email
  • AI-based automations and bots
  • Calendars, tasks and self-service meeting scheduling
  • Project, inventory and supplier management
  • Connection to WordPress sites and to other systems

Made in Israel

Why an Israeli CRM system

An Israeli business works with Israeli payment processing, with Israeli accounting and with customers who speak Hebrew. A system that was built here is already connected to all of that.

  • Fully Hebrew A system built in Hebrew, right to left, for businesses that work in Israel.

  • Truly personal customization The fields, the screens and the processes are built around the way the business works, and not the other way around.
  • Personal service Support in Hebrew, and a system that is updated and improved regularly.

Frequently asked questions

Questions and answers about Kala CRM

What is Kala CRM?

Kala CRM is an Israeli system for managing, marketing and automating the business: inquiries, customers, sales, billing, calendars and communication with customers in one system, fully in Hebrew and customized.

Who is Kala CRM for?

Israeli companies and businesses that want a CRM system in Hebrew, personal service, and processes tailored exactly to the way they work.

What is the difference between Kala CRM and international CRM systems?

Kala is a system made in Israel: it is built in Hebrew, connected to the Israeli providers (payment processing, accounting, phone systems, Masav and WhatsApp), and can be deeply customized, with local service.

Which systems does Kala CRM connect to?

Credit card processing and digital wallets, accounting systems, phone systems (PBX), Masav (Israel's bank clearing system), WhatsApp, SMS and email, WordPress sites, and other systems through custom integrations.

Can Kala CRM be adapted to a process that is unique to the business?

Yes, and that is the heart of the system. You can build almost any process you can imagine in it, and I specify and build it together with the business.

Does Kala CRM have artificial intelligence?

Yes. The system includes AI-based automations and bots, which answer inquiries and make service and sales more efficient.

What is my connection to Kala CRM?

I have accompanied the development of Kala CRM from its first day until today. I specify, set up and implement the system in companies, and support them over time.

How much does a Kala CRM implementation cost?

The price depends on the scope of the implementation, so it is not published on the site. The first consultation is free.
