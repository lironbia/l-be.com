Privacy

Privacy policy

In short: the site does not track you. It has no cookies, no tracking tools and no advertising. The only information that reaches me is what you chose to send in a form.

Who is responsible for the information

The site l-be.com is operated by Liron Bialkovich (L-Be), an independent business in Israel. For any question or request about privacy, you can reach me through the contact form.

What the site does not do

  • Does not store cookies.
  • Does not use tracking or statistics tools, or advertising pixels.
  • Does not build a profile of visitors, and does not make automated decisions about them.
  • Does not sell information and does not pass it on to advertisers.
  • Does not load fonts or code from other companies' servers. Everything is served from the site itself.

What information is collected, and when

When you send a form

  • Contact form: name, business name, WhatsApp number, email and message. Only name and WhatsApp are required.
  • "Email me so I can come back and read about you later": email address only.
  • Accessibility inquiry form: name, email or phone, and a description of the inquiry.
  • The form for AI agents: the name of the person on whose behalf the request is made, a way to get in touch and the content of the request.

Along with every form, the following are also sent: the address of the page it was sent from, the page from which you arrived at the site, and campaign details if there were any in the link you came through.

What is stored only on your device

The settings you chose in the accessibility menu, your high score in the game on the error page, and when a form was last sent from this browser (to prevent duplicate submissions). This information is stored in your browser only, and is not sent to me.

Site hosting

The site is hosted with an external hosting provider (GitHub). Like any web server, it receives the IP address and technical details of the browser in order to display the page, and may keep them in security logs. I do not have access to those logs.

External services that open only on a click

Clicking "Book a call" opens a Calendly window, an external service with its own cookies and privacy policy. Links to other sites, such as LinkedIn, lead to sites with policies of their own.

What I use the information for, and on what basis

  • To respond to your inquiry and prepare a proposal: steps toward entering into a contract, at your request.
  • To send the "Read later" email: based on your consent. It is one email.
  • To handle accessibility inquiries: a legal obligation, and a legitimate interest in improving the site.

I do not use the details for marketing mailings without explicit consent.

Who receives the information

Only service providers that process it for me: an automation service that passes the form on to me, my email service and my customer management system. Some of the providers store information on servers in the European Union and in the United States.

How long the information is kept

For as long as it is needed to handle the inquiry and for the business relationship that follows from it. You can ask me to delete it at any moment.

Your rights

  • To receive a copy of the information I hold about you.
  • To correct inaccurate information.
  • To request deletion of the information.
  • To restrict the use of the information, or to object to it.
  • To receive the information in a file that can be transferred to another party.
  • To withdraw consent you have given, at any moment.

To exercise a right, write to me in the contact form. I will reply within a month. You can also file a complaint with Israel's Privacy Protection Authority, or with the data protection authority in your country in the European Union.

Information security

The site itself is a static site: it has no database and no personal area, so no personal information is stored on it. The forms are sent over an encrypted connection. The information is kept with well-known providers, and access to it is restricted.

No system is one hundred percent protected. If a security incident occurs that affects your information, I will notify you.

Minors

The site is intended for businesses, and is not directed at children.

Changes to the policy

If the policy changes, for example if a statistics tool is added to the site, this page will be updated before the change.
