The L-Be System

The operating system of your business. With AI inside.

One place where people and AI agents work together. Every area has an owner, and at any moment you can see what is happening and what is waiting for your decision.

The AI proposes. A person decides. The business moves forward.

Who it is for

Not one more tool. The way your business works.

For company owners and managers who want a business that runs by a method, even when they are not in the room.

Every client gets the system, and the L-Be team builds on it what their business needs.

What sets it apart

A system that starts from the question "what does it take to run a business"

Most systems start from customers and try to expand. L-Be starts from the business itself.

  • Compared with a CRM A CRM system knows who your customers are. L-Be knows how your business works: who is responsible, what is stuck, who decides.
  • Compared with regular BI BI shows you what happened. L-Be also makes sure someone does something about it.
  • Compared with a general AI chat A chat answers a question and forgets. In L-Be an AI agent has a role, permissions, someone responsible above it and a record of every action.

Core components

What is in the system

Some of the components are built into the system, and some are built for you by L-Be, according to what the business needs.

  • Built into the system

    Project management and business development

    The roadmap, sprints and tasks on one screen, with a clear "Waiting for you".

    For example: you open it in the morning and see what is urgent, what is next and what needs you.

  • Built into the system

    Management: seats and responsibility

    Every role has a holder, a person or an AI agent, and every area of the business has an owner.

    For example: you see right away which area has been left without an owner.

  • Built into the system

    Blockers and escalation

    Nothing gets stuck quietly. Every delay reaches the one who decides.

    For example: an AI agent stops at a business decision, and it goes up to the right manager.

  • Built into the system

    Approval gates

    What matters does not happen without your approval, and everything is recorded.

    For example: a significant change does not go live without a sign-off.

  • Built into the system

    The setup wizard

    The business defines itself: identity, vision, goals, way of working.

    For example: at the end of the wizard there is a written master plan.

  • Built in, and adapted for you

    Data repository

    All of the business's information in one place, organized and linked.

    For example: a customer, a task, a decision and its owner are connected to each other.

  • Built in, and extended for you

    Knowledge base

    The procedures, the decisions and the documents open next to the work they belong to.

    For example: whoever carries out a task sees the decision behind it.

  • Built in, and dedicated dashboards are built for you

    Dashboards

    A status picture for the manager, and for every seat its own picture.

    For example: a "manager's view" of the sprint and of the blockers.

  • Built for you

    BI

    The business's data turns into answers.

    For example: where the bottleneck is, and what has changed since last month.

  • Built and configured for you

    AI traffic management

    All of the business's AI under one control.

    For example: you see who uses AI, for what purpose, and how much it costs.

  • A built-in interface, connections built for you

    Interfaces and integrations

    The system talks to the tools you already have, and to AI agents.

    For example: an AI agent opens a task and reports through a secure interface.

  • Built for you

    AI-based web applications

    From an idea to a screen you can see, and from there to a tool that works.

    For example: you ask in words, get a clickable example, and approve.

  • Built into the system

    Secure sign-in and permissions

    Everyone sees only what their role allows.

    For example: two-step sign-in for every user.

AI traffic management

AI in the business with no surprises on the bill and no decisions that nobody approved

Today every employee uses a different AI, on a different account, without anyone knowing what was sent, how much it costs and what came back. L-Be puts one gate between the business and all the AI models.

  • Control Who uses AI, for what purpose, and with which model.
  • Cost How much the AI costs the business, in dollars, by usage.
  • Budget and brake A monthly ceiling, and an off switch when needed.
  • The right model for the task A strong model for a complex decision, an economical model for routine work.
  • Trust in stages The AI starts as an observer, continues as an adviser, and acts on its own only after it has proven itself.
  • No dependence on one provider The business is not locked into one model or one company.

Security

AI proposes, a person decides on everything that cannot be undone

This is the principle above everything else. And these are the things behind it:

  • The system runs on L-Be's dedicated cloud server, not on shared hosting.
  • All traffic is encrypted, and the database is closed to the internet.
  • Two-step sign-in for every user: a password and a personal code from an authenticator app.
  • Passwords, authentication secrets and AI keys are kept protected and encrypted.
  • Permissions by role, and full separation between one organization and another.
  • A record of who did what, and when.
  • Automatic daily backup, with restore tests.
  • Every update to the system goes through automated tests, and is rolled back automatically if something is wrong.

What implementation looks like

Six steps, from definition to a system that works

  1. Defining the business A short wizard: who we are, where we are going, how we work. At the end there is a master plan.
  2. L-Be sets up the environment In the background, with no need to wait for it.
  3. Defining people and roles Who holds each seat, what each one sees, who decides on what.
  4. The team joins in stages Everyone gets an introduction to the system and to their role in it.
  5. Kickoff Everyone aligns on one plan and gets to work.
  6. L-Be builds for you Dashboards, connections and tools, in the order of the business's priorities.

What is needed from the business: one person who leads the system on the business's side, and honest answers to the wizard's questions.

Frequently asked questions

Questions and answers about L-Be

What is L-Be?

L-Be is the operating system of the business: one place where people and AI agents work together, every area has an owner, and at any moment you can see what is happening and what is waiting for a decision.

Does L-Be replace the CRM or the accounting software?

No. L-Be connects to the tools you already have. A CRM system knows who your customers are, and L-Be knows how your business works: who is responsible, what is stuck, who decides.

Who decides, the AI or a person?

AI proposes, a person decides on everything that cannot be undone. The AI starts as an observer, continues as an adviser, and acts on its own only after it has proven itself.

How do you control the cost of the AI?

Through AI traffic management: you see how much the AI costs the business by usage, set a monthly ceiling, and there is an off switch when needed.

What is needed from the business to get started?

One person who leads the system on the business's side, and honest answers to the questions of the setup wizard.

Can we build on the system ourselves?

Today every client gets the system, and the L-Be team builds on it what the business needs. Later on, the option to develop on the system will open to the clients themselves too.

How much does it cost?

The price is not published on the site. The first consultation is free.
