About the system

What is GoHighLevel?

GoHighLevel, also called HighLevel or GHL for short, is one platform for managing sales, marketing and service. Instead of several separate tools that don't talk to each other, everything sits in one place.

  • CRM and sales funnels (Pipelines)
  • One conversations inbox: WhatsApp, SMS, email, Facebook and Instagram
  • Automations (Workflows)
  • Calendars and appointment booking
  • Forms, landing pages and websites
  • Email and message campaigns
  • AI agents for chat and phone conversations
  • Reports and dashboards

GHL in Israel

What you should know before implementing GHL in Israel

The system was built for the American market, so implementing it in Israel requires a few adjustments. These are the things I configure in every setup.

  • Hebrew and RTL Forms, messages, pages and automations are built in Hebrew, aligned to the right.
  • WhatsApp In Israel it is the main communication channel. It is connected to the conversations inbox and to the automations.
  • Telephony and messaging Israeli numbers and sending messages require correct configuration, or a connection to a local provider.
  • Payment processing and invoices Connected to the Israeli systems the business already works with.
  • Time zone, currency and language Set in advance in every account, so that the calendars, the prices and the messages come out right.

Frequently asked questions

Questions and answers about GHL

What is GoHighLevel (GHL)?

GoHighLevel is one platform for managing sales, marketing and service: CRM, sales funnels, a conversations inbox, automations, calendars, forms, landing pages and AI agents. It is also called HighLevel, or GHL for short.

Is GHL suitable for businesses in Israel?

Yes, with adjustments. The system was built for the American market, so Hebrew, WhatsApp, telephony, payment processing and invoices need to be configured correctly. That is the part I do.

Can you work with GHL in Hebrew?

Everything the customers see is built in Hebrew: forms, messages, pages and automations. The language of the admin interface itself depends on what HighLevel supports.

What is the difference between a regular account and an agency account?

An agency account manages several sub-accounts, one for each client or business. That way an agency can set up a system for each client, clone ready-made templates and brand the system under its own name.

I already have a GHL account. Can I get help with something specific?

Yes. You can start with one session of a few hours: we go through the system together, and afterwards comes a summary report with recommendations and a work plan.

Can I move to GHL from another CRM system?

In most cases, yes. In the specification call we check which data and processes move over, and how.

How much does it cost?

The price depends on the scope of the implementation, so it is not published on the site. The first consultation is free.
