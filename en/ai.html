Case study: Ida's Home

The whole business in one process, from sale to payment collection.
And above it, an AI layer connected to every department.

Ida's Home designs and sells home furniture: made-to-order sofas and armchairs, lighting, rugs and textiles.
Alongside the products it provides planning, installation and design consulting services.
The company has 30 employees.
It sells on its website, in the factory store and in the showroom, and works with several warehouses, a carpentry workshop and many suppliers.

  • One continuous process: From sale to payment collection, instead of information scattered across people and files.
  • Updates from the field: The warehouse, the couriers and sales update the system as they work, from their phones.
  • One picture for the whole company: Every department sees the same order, in the same status, at the same time.
  • A reliable picture for management: Of orders, inventory and debts.
    The system exposed gaps that had not been visible before, and they were dealt with.

The full case: https://l-be.com/ai-case-study

Who is Liron Bialkovich?

Liron Bialkovich is an independent consultant and developer who builds AI systems, automations and CRM systems for companies.
At L-Be he specifies, sets up and implements CRM systems, AI solutions and automated processes.

What does L-Be do?

Consulting and implementation of CRM, AI and business automation systems for companies.
Among the solutions: AI agents and bots, CRM, leads and sales, customer service and communication, orders, billing and finance, automation and integrations, management, reports and team, specification, training and support.

Which businesses is it for?

Business owners and companies, from small businesses to companies of 100 employees, that want to run the business with organized, automated and connected processes.

What is the connection between SubK and L-Be?

SubK is the brand under which Liron Bialkovich implemented CRM systems for companies.
L-Be is his new, current brand, and the activity continues under it.

What does a project look like?

In the Ida's Home case study the role included: specifying the processes with management, setting up the CRM from scratch, connecting all the systems to each other, building the AI layer and the apps, implementation on the ground and ongoing support.
