The L-Be System

AI-based business systems: a dedicated, secure server for your business

A dedicated, secure, AI-based server with a business development environment.
It complements the management and operations systems the business already has, and is customized to its needs.

Free first consultation Book a call (opens in a pop-up window)

The idea

Your systems stay. Above them, one layer is built that knows the whole business.

The CRM, the accounting and the inventory keep working as usual.
The server connects to them, brings their data together, and runs AI on top of them.
On the same environment we build what the business is missing: reports, dashboards and apps.

Core components

What is implemented in every business

Every implementation is customized, but these are the components at the base of each one.

  • Central data repository

    The data from all the systems of the business is gathered in one place, with one agreed business definition for every number.

  • BI system

    You ask in plain language, and get an answer based on the company's data.
    A useful report is saved, and runs again with one click.

  • Dashboards

    Dashboards for every role.
    You ask for a dashboard in words, and the system builds it.

  • Project management for business development

    A roadmap, decisions and resolutions, sprints, tasks and milestones, in one place.

  • Knowledge base

    The procedures, decisions and documents of the business in one place, which the AI reads from too.

  • AI traffic management

    Central control over the use of AI: who asks, what they are allowed to see, and what passes through the system.

  • Interfaces and integrations

    A connection to the systems already working in the business, so that information flows between them by itself.

  • Development of AI-based web apps

    Dedicated business apps, for example for the warehouse, the field or management, built on the same environment and on the same data.

Security and control

AI that works within clear boundaries

An AI system connected to a company's data has to be secure and controlled.
This is how it's built.

  • A dedicated server for the business, not a shared environment
  • The AI suggests, and a person approves
  • Every employee sees only what their role allows
  • Sign-in uses two-step verification
  • Customers' personal details do not leave the company
  • Every action is logged

Frequently asked questions

Questions and answers about the system

What is an AI-based business system?

A dedicated, secure server for the business, which brings together the data from all its systems and runs AI on top of them.
On the same environment we build reports, dashboards and business apps, customized to the needs of the business.

Does it replace the CRM or the accounting system?

No.
The system complements the management and operations systems the business already has: it connects to them, brings their data together and adds an AI layer on top of them.

Where is the data stored?

On a server dedicated to the business.
Access to it is by two-step verification and according to the permissions of each role, and every action is logged.

What can the AI do?

Answer questions in plain language based on the company's data, build reports and dashboards from a request in words, and run business apps.
The AI suggests, and a person approves.

Who is it for?

Companies whose data is scattered across several systems, and that want one picture of the business and tools that are built as needed.
The case study on the site describes a company of 30 employees.

Is the system customized for every business?

Yes.
There are core components that are implemented in every business, such as the data repository, the BI, the dashboards and the knowledge base, and on top of them we build what the business needs.

How much does it cost?

The price depends on the scope of the implementation, so it is not published on the site.
The first consultation is free.
