01

AI agents and bots

Artificial intelligence that works inside the processes of the business, not next to them.

Service and sales bot on WhatsApp

Answers inquiries around the clock, collects the details, and passes on to the team only what requires a person.

An AI agent on the business's data

You ask in plain language, and get an answer, a report or a dashboard based on the company's data.

Analysis of incoming inquiries

Emails and messages become organized leads, with the details in the right fields.

Responsible AI

The AI suggests and a person approves, with role-based permissions and a record of every action.

02

CRM, leads and sales

Every inquiry arrives in one place, and none gets lost.

Lead collection and management

All the inquiries, from all the sources, come into one system and reach whoever needs to handle them.

A complete customer file

Every customer's calls, messages, orders and documents, in one place.

Sales funnels and workflows

Statuses, handling scripts and tasks, so that no inquiry falls through the cracks.

Quotes and follow-up

A designed quote goes out in seconds, and the follow-up runs by itself.

Marketing measurement

You see which campaign brought the person who paid in the end, and not just the one who left their details.

03

Customer service and communication

Every channel your customers use, documented and connected.

WhatsApp, SMS, email and social networks

Outgoing and incoming messages from all the channels, recorded in the customer file.

Cloud phone system (PBX)

An incoming call opens the customer card, and the recording is saved in it.

Meeting scheduling and attendance confirmations

Customers pick an available time themselves, and the reminders go out on their own.

Satisfaction surveys

You ask the customers in a form or a bot, and the answers come back into the system.

Personal area for customers and suppliers

Viewing data and carrying out actions, without picking up the phone.

04

Orders, billing and finance

From the deal to payment collection, in one process.

Order management

Products, operations, shipping and payment, from the deal to delivery.

Payment processing and billing

Credit cards, digital wallets and direct debits, straight from the system.

Subscriptions and recurring billing

Retainers and subscriptions are charged automatically, with tracking of what was not collected.

Accounting documents

Invoices and receipts come out of the process itself, and are synced with accounting.

Inventory, purchasing and suppliers

Catalog, warehouses and purchase orders, connected to the customers' orders.

05

Automation and integrations

What repeats itself moves into the system, and separate systems start talking to each other.

Process automation

Tasks, alerts, messages and status updates happen by themselves, at the right time.

Connection to any system with an API

Information moves between the business's systems without double entry.

Stores and websites

Orders and inquiries from the website and the online store go straight into the system.

Smart forms

Registration, signing a document, updating details and purchasing, in a form that updates the system.

Shipping companies

A shipment is opened from within the order, and its tracking comes back to it.

06

Management, reports and team

One picture of the business, one you can rely on.

Dashboards and reports

An up-to-date picture of the business, tailored to each role.

Task and project management

Who does what and by when, with a calendar and progress tracking.

Work hours tracking

Hours by customer, project or order, to know how much the work really costs.

Procedures and protocols

Written work procedures, to train employees quickly and grow without chaos.

Process specification

Specification of complex systems

From an idea to a plan you can build from.

Training and implementation on the ground

Ongoing support

Consulting for companies
